Takeaways (directional, because with n = 5 one QA item = 20 points):
1. **Via English (M3) is the strongest general method:** 56–66% cheaper and ≥ 95% quality in 5 of 6 cells. The Tamil-QA miss is one item where even M0 is only "partial".
2. **M5 is cheapest (63–75%)** and holds for Hindi, but loses 7–11% on Kannada and Tamil, so whether it passes depends on the language.
3. **Token pruning (M2) is safe at 80%, language-dependent at 60%, and breaks Kannada/Tamil QA at 40%.** Hindi tolerates heavy pruning; Dravidian languages don't
   (agglutinative words → pruning removes meaning-bearing pieces).
4. **M6 works for summaries:** ~20–30% saving with no quality loss. It's an output-side win that stacks with input methods.
5. **M4 ITRANS is a negative result on DeepSeek;** keep it only for the GLM test.
6. Oracle choice at τ = 95% (router upper bound): hi QA M5 70% · hi summ M5 66% · kn QA M3 66% · kn summ M3 59% · ta QA M2_80 18% · ta summ M3 60%.
